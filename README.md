# Gas Sweetening Process Using MDEA Solvent

**Aspen HYSYS | Natural Gas Processing | MDEA | Process Simulation**

---

## Overview

This project demonstrates the **steady-state simulation of a natural gas sweetening process using MDEA (Methyldiethanolamine) solvent in Aspen HYSYS**.

The objective is to remove acidic gases, primarily **Hydrogen Sulfide (H₂S)** and **Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)**, from sour natural gas through an amine absorption and regeneration process.

---

## Project Objectives

* Develop a natural gas sweetening flowsheet using **Aspen HYSYS**.
* Model **H₂S and CO₂ absorption** using MDEA solvent.
* Simulate **rich MDEA regeneration** and lean solvent recycle.
* Perform **material and energy balance** analysis.
* Analyse key process streams and operating conditions.

---

## Process Description

The process consists of two main sections:

### 1. Gas Absorption

Sour natural gas enters the **MDEA absorber** and contacts lean MDEA solvent.

H₂S and CO₂ are absorbed into the liquid MDEA phase, producing:

* **Sweet Gas** — treated natural gas leaving the absorber.
* **Rich MDEA** — solvent containing absorbed acid gases.

### 2. MDEA Regeneration

Rich MDEA is sent to the **regeneration section**, where heat supplied through the reboiler releases the absorbed acid gases.

The regenerated **lean MDEA** is cooled and recycled back to the absorber.

### Process Flow

**Sour Gas → MDEA Absorber → Sweet Gas**

**Rich MDEA → Heat Exchanger → MDEA Regenerator → Lean MDEA → Cooler → Absorber**
